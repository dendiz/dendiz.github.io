> PolyGate is a data aggregator running on the Prefect task orchestration platform.

PolyGate currently ingests
- USD/TRY prices
- Crude oil futures prices
- US CPI data (Seasonally adjusted and non adjusted)

PolyGate will ingest
- Alpaca.ai US stock market data for all available symbols
- Alpaca.ai US options data
